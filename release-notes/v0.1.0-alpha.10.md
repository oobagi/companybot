Alpha build — expect rough edges.

## What's new
- **A new app layout.** A rail on the left for Chat, Drive, Databases, Agents and Customize, your own pins, a workspace switcher in the title bar, and ⌘1–9 / ⌥1–9 to jump around. Your rail and sidebar layout follow your account to every device. (#184, #202, #242, #256)
- **Agents that know when to speak.** In channels, agents decide whether to reply, react, or stay quiet, and you can see what they're doing live under the composer. Type while one is replying to steer it, or stop it mid-reply. A burst of messages gets one answer. (#166, #179, #165, #193, #192, #191)
- **Agents working together.** Agents can hand work to each other and check back later on their own. They pause if they keep replying to each other, and you choose to let them continue or stop them. (#168, #256)
- **Who can access what.** Custom roles, and per-channel, database and page access (view or edit). Agent replies that use something private stay private, with a Share button for just the answer. (#204, #208, #210, #233)
- **Personal or team skills**, and **custom connectors**: add your own MCP server. (#207, #256)
- **Richer chat.** See who reacted, clickable @names, #channels and /skills, and files agents can use and save anywhere. (#185, #206, #195)

## Improvements
- Cleaner screens: consistent buttons, dialogs with sticky headers and footers, filters in the top bar, centered empty states, and forms that keep unsaved edits when you leave. (#249, #250, #256, #258)
- Faster, steadier chat in big workspaces. (#237, #240, #232)

## Fixes
- Agents no longer answer twice at once, speak for people, or lock their replies by mistake. (#256)
- Many fixes from testers to access, approvals and private replies. (#251, #239)
