Alpha build — expect rough edges.

## What's new
- **Companybot says hello.** It introduces itself in #general when you make a workspace and asks about your company one question at a time. People who join get a short welcome in their DM with it. A tutorial above the message box shows the basics, and memory saves show right in the chat. (#74, #82)
- **Switch workspaces from a rail.** Every workspace you're in sits at the far left with unread dots and mention counts. Jump between them with ⌘1–9, accept invites there, or press + to make a new one. (#81)
- **Nested pages with pictures.** Put pages inside pages, move them around in the sidebar tree, and paste, drag or `/image` pictures into them. (#64, #73)
- **Board view for databases.** Switch any view between Table and Board, grouped by a choice field. Records now open as pages. (#62, #72)
- **Pictures for everyone.** Add a workspace logo, your profile photo and agent pictures. (#70)
- **Team memory anyone can fix.** Everyone can add, edit and delete team memories, with history to undo mistakes. (#69)
- **Typing indicators.** See who's typing, people and agents, under the message box. (#67)
- **Pick any AI model for an agent** from hundreds of models that support tools. (#57)
- **Delete goes to Trash** for agents, skills and automations too. Anyone who could delete something can restore it. (#59, #71)

## Improvements
- **A cleaner app.** One top bar on every screen, agents listed with your DMs, Settings holds Trash and Customize, consistent pickers and empty states, and content fades under the bars. (#58, #65, #66, #72)
- **New workspaces start simple:** just #general and Companybot. (#60)
- **Faster chat.** Streaming replies no longer slow down the rest of the app, and the chat list loads less. (#80)
