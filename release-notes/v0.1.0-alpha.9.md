Alpha build — expect rough edges.

## What's new
- **Web search.** Agents look things up and read what they find, including PDFs and spreadsheets. (#101, #154)
- **Attach files.** Add files and pictures to a message, and agents read them. (#102, #111)
- **Pictures.** Agents make and edit images. (#104, #151)
- **Side chats.** Start a separate conversation with an agent next to its main DM. (#112)
- **Smarter composer.** @ people and agents, # channels, / skills, plus emoji, drafts and dictation. (#89, #100)
- **Usage.** Settings → Usage shows what AI costs by month, agent, person and model. (#97, #146, #155)
- **Help.** Help docs in the app, and agents can answer how-do-I questions. (#98)
- **Link previews** under chat messages. (#103)

## Improvements
- New workspaces start with a short setup: your name, the company and who to invite. (#88, #84, #99, #105)
- Each agent has a Reasoning setting, Medium by default. The default model is now GPT-6 Luna, which costs far less. (#152, #85)
- The sidebar folds, reorders and resizes, and owners choose how long threads stay in it. (#86, #112, #147, #149)
- A title bar with back, forward, a sidebar toggle and Help. (#83)

## Fixes
- @mentions in threads now notify people and open the thread. (#92)
- Agent replies always finish, even after a lot of research. (#154)
- Drafts stay with the right account, and pasted text and attached files survive switching chats. (#145, #142)
- Dictation records once per click and turns the mic off when you leave. (#139)
