Alpha build — expect rough edges.

## What's new
- **Clients, Deals, Tasks and Calls.** Real lists and record pages with filters, sorting, custom fields, links between records, full history and undo. Agents can look up and update records from chat. (#28, #30, #33, #34)
- **Invite people for real.** Invites now send an email, and you can belong to several workspaces and switch between them. (#35)
- **Notifications.** Desktop notifications and a dock badge for DMs and mentions, with a setting for how much you get. (#38)
- **Profile cards.** Click a name or avatar to see someone's role and local time, or message them. (#40)
- **Agents ask before acting outside Companybot.** Sending, texting, paying or changing another app shows the exact action with Allow once / Always allow / Deny. Owners can lock risky actions for everyone. (#20, #27, #32)
- **Make your own automations and skills.** Anyone can create them in Customize or just by asking in chat. Automations are opt-in, can post in a channel, and can start from a schedule, a webhook, a new message or a new member. "Plugins" are now called Connectors. (#17, #18, #19, #22)
- **Private memories.** What agents remember about you is visible only to you, and owners can turn agent memory off for the workspace. (#42)
- **Delete a workspace or your account** from Settings. (#46, #47)

## Fixes
- Amounts keep their own currency and are stored exactly. (#29, #31)
- Clearer chat: readable dates, specific tool lines, and no repeating what a table already shows. (#39)
- Removing someone from a workspace now removes everything that was theirs, including their connections and private automations. (#46)
