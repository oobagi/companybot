## What's new
- **Databases.** Make your own, like Projects or Properties, next to Clients, Deals, Tasks and Calls. Save filters as views your team shares, shown as tabs. (#50)
- **Pages.** Notion-style docs in the sidebar, and a page on every client, deal and task for notes. Several people can edit at once, and page history shows who changed what ("Edited by Companybot, for Dana") with one-click restore. (#51)
- **Trash.** Deleting records, databases, fields, pages or channels moves them to Trash for 30 days, so you can restore them, links and all. Owners can delete forever. (#53)
- **Agents can do what you can.** Ask Companybot to post in a channel, DM someone, manage channels, edit automations and skills, invite people, change fields, read history and restore page versions. Anything that changes who has power stays with people. (#52, #55)

## Improvements
- Automations someone else made ask you the first time they want to act as you, like posting or reading your DMs, and only that step waits. Agent edits to skills and invites ask first. Approval cards name who's affected. (#54)
- Remove someone from a channel from its details. (#54)
